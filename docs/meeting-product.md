# Product: governance outcomes from Teams meetings

roleALPHA Sessions closes one gap: what a Teams meeting decides about the organisation's governance should reach roleALPHA quickly, reviewed by people and decided there. Everything else a meeting needs, Microsoft Teams already brings.

## Who does what

| Step | Where | Who |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Invitation | Outlook or Teams calendar; the app is added as a tab | Organiser |
| Agenda, tensions, notes | Loop meeting notes in the invitation; each agenda item is a tension | Everybody invited |
| Meeting | Teams, with the Loop notes, optionally the Facilitator and transcription | Everybody |
| Outcomes into roleALPHA | The roleALPHA Sessions side panel, or the Copilot agent | Note-taker |
| Proposals | rALPH, roleALPHA's assistant with its governance skills | rALPH, checked by the note-taker |
| Decision | roleALPHA: liberal modules at once, consent or consensus for the rest | Participants or affected persons, as roleALPHA is configured |
| Information | Summary in the meeting chat | Note-taker sends it |

## Principles

- **Teams is the meeting, roleALPHA is the governance.** The app stores nothing itself: one roleALPHA scenario per meeting holds its drafts.
- **rALPH proposes, people decide.** Every proposal is shown with its reason and can be edited; only adopted ones become drafts, and drafts take effect only after approval in roleALPHA.
- **Existing entries can be changed directly.** The note-taker can pick any entry they may see in roleALPHA, change its fields and adopt the change as a draft of the meeting, without going through rALPH.
- **Liberal is fast, consent is fair.** Modules roleALPHA approves liberally need no vote. For the others, a module can let the meeting's participants vote, so that the people who were there decide and an absence does not block a change.
- **No Copilot needed.** The side panel works for everybody; people with a Copilot licence can additionally use the agent, which reads the Loop notes itself.

## Not part of this version

- Reading the whole transcript with rALPH: rALPH accepts short notes per agenda item today; a larger input needs a change in roleALPHA.
- Reading the Loop notes from the side panel: Microsoft offers no supported interface for Loop content. The Copilot agent reads them through Copilot.
